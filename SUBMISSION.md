# Submission

**Panta Intelligence** turns Panta prediction-market infrastructure into an intelligence surface. Instead of forcing users to browse markets one by one, it ranks them using a transparent score combining price conviction, liquidity, activity and recent YES/NO flow.

The MVP integrates Panta's market catalog, market detail and market trade tape. It is intentionally read-only so it can be judged safely without wallets or real funds.

Future extensions: unusual-activity alerts, historical snapshots, agent-readable JSON feeds and embeddable market-intelligence widgets.
